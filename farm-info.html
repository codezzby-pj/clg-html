Farm information

  • Irrigation Workflow
    1. Collect Farm Data – Enter the farm/plot details and crop information.
    2. Monitor Soil Moisture – Check the soil moisture level using sensors.
    3. Check Weather Conditions – Consider the current weather and forecast.
    4. Analyze Crop Stage – Identify the current growth stage of the sugarcane.
    5. Generate Irrigation Recommendation – Use the available information to determine whether irrigation is required.
    6. Apply Required Irrigation – Irrigate the field according to the recommendation.
    7. Monitor Again – Recheck soil moisture and field conditions after irrigation.
  • Sensors Used-
  • Soil Moisture Sensor
  • Temperature Sensor
  • Humidity Sensor
  • Rainfall Sensor
