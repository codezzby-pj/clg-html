Popular Features of our site ---

Our Smart Irrigation Advisory Web Portal is designed to help sugarcane farmers make better irrigation decisions using AI and sensor-based information. The website provides useful features such as farm registration, soil moisture monitoring, weather information, irrigation recommendations, crop information, and farm plot navigation. It also helps users access organized agricultural information through an easy-to-use interface. The portal can be useful for farmers, agricultural officers, researchers, and sugar factory management by providing timely and understandable information for efficient water management.

Problems faced by farmers while farming sugarcane--

Sugarcane farmers often face challenges such as improper irrigation, changing weather conditions, water shortage, and difficulty in monitoring soil moisture. These problems can affect crop growth and reduce productivity. Our smart irrigation system helps farmers make better irrigation decisions using soil, weather, and crop information.

Solutions to those problem--

Our smart irrigation system helps farmers overcome these challenges by providing irrigation recommendations based on soil moisture, weather conditions, and crop requirements. It also provides crop information and advisory services, helping farmers use water efficiently and make informed decisions for better crop growth.

Some farming tips while farming sugarcane--

Farmers should regularly monitor soil moisture and weather conditions to avoid over-irrigation or water shortage. Proper irrigation at different crop stages, timely monitoring of the field, and following suitable crop advisory can help maintain healthy sugarcane growth and improve water efficiency.
